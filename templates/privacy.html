Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2025

Overview

The Medical Research Question Framing Tool is designed to help you structure medical research questions. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use this tool.

What Information We Collect

When you use this tool, we process:

  • Your question text: The medical research question you submit through the form
  • Technical metadata: Timestamps, response types, and processing telemetry for system monitoring

We do not collect: Personal identifying information, IP addresses, cookies, or tracking data.

How We Use Your Information

Your question text is processed to:

  • Check if it's within scope (medical research topic, English language)
  • Assess whether it's well-structured or needs clarification
  • Generate rewrite suggestions if needed
  • Detect potential emergency language for safety warnings

This processing happens in real-time through API calls to language model providers. Your question is sent to these providers solely for analysis and is not retained by them for training purposes.

Data Storage and Retention

Your questions are not stored in our database.

  • Processing only: Questions are processed in memory and discarded after the response is generated
  • Telemetry logs: Non-identifying metadata (timestamps, response types, processing times) may be logged for system monitoring and performance improvement
  • No long-term retention: We do not maintain a database of submitted questions or responses

Third-Party Services

This tool uses language models accessed through OpenRouter API. When you submit a question:

  • Your question text is sent to the API provider for processing
  • The provider processes your question according to their privacy policy
  • Your data is not used for AI model training
  • Responses are returned to you and not retained by the provider

For more information, see OpenRouter's Privacy Policy.

Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure API authentication with third-party services
  • Input validation to prevent malicious inputs
  • No storage of sensitive medical information

Important Disclaimers

This tool is not a medical service

This tool does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It only helps you frame research questions. Do not submit personal health information or use this tool for medical emergencies.

If you're experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately. For health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare provider.

Your Rights

Because we do not store your questions:

  • There is no personal data to request access to or deletion of
  • Each question you submit is processed independently
  • You can stop using the tool at any time

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. Continued use of the tool after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please contact the tool administrator.
