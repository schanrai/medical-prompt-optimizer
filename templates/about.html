About

Why this tool exists and how it works.

What this is

A tool that turns vague or risky medical questions into clearer, research-oriented prompts you can use in ChatGPT, Claude, or similar tools. It does not give medical advice or diagnose. It only helps you ask better questions.

Why it exists

LLMs are good at answering when the question is clear and scoped; they're bad when the question is vague or sounds like “tell me what to do.” Small wording changes can change the answer (e.g. “rest at home” vs “go to ER”). This tool helps you ask in a way that's safer and more useful.

By refining your question before you paste it into a chatbot, you get research-oriented prompts instead of advice-style answers.

The problem we're addressing

Over 40 million people use ChatGPT for health information every day. Hallucinations are a real problem: studies report hallucination rates from about 1.5% to 23% on general medical queries, depending on task complexity. That can lead to harmful or incorrect medical information.

Research shows that better prompting can reduce hallucinations—for example, from 66% to 44% in one study (Communications Medicine). How you ask the question affects how reliable the answer is.

People researching health conditions often have domain knowledge but rarely have training in research methodology. They may not know which details—population, comparison, outcome, or context—actually matter when framing a question for an LLM.

What it does

This tool encourages questions that are grounded in what can be known and verified, rather than vague or advice-seeking. That helps you get more objective, less biased answers from the model.

  • Checks if the question is medical and in scope
  • Clarifies missing pieces (e.g. population, comparison)
  • Either gives you a ready-to-use prompt or suggests clarifications

FAQ

Does it give medical advice?
No. It only refines your question so you can use it in an LLM. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
Can I use the output in ChatGPT / Claude?
Yes. The output is a prompt you can copy and paste into any chatbot.
What happens to my question?
It is processed to run the pipeline (scope check, clarification, etc.). We do not store your questions. See the Privacy page for details.
Why was I shown crisis resources?
The question contained language we treat as crisis-related. We show support resources (e.g. 988, SAMHSA) and do not answer as if it were a normal research question.
